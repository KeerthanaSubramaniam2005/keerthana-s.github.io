ABOUT

A mechanical engineer's approach — model it, understand how it's made, then make it work.

I'm a Mechanical Engineering student at Kangeyam Institute of Technology, Tirupur, with hands-on experience across CAD modelling, 2D drafting, product design, fabrication, manufacturing processes and quality inspection — built through internships, academic projects and technical activities.
